I am a PhD researcher in Social Statistics at the University of Manchester. My doctoral research focuses on developing causal inference methods for evaluating health policies and interventions. I aim to integrate my expertise in handling complex routine datasets with sophisticated causal modelling to drive evidence-based economic and social care policy. I specialise in doubly robust machine learning estimators for confounding adjustment, enabling the generalisation of policy/intervention effects to target populations. This builds on my management of national data infrastructure at Chile's National Institute of Statistics, where I directed the national agricultural statistics program.

Research Interests
======
- Causal inference models
- Econometric methods
- Epidemiological research
- Complex survey design
- Experimental design
