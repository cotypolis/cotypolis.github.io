Hello, I'm Constanza! 👋

I am a PhD Candidate in [Social Statistics at the University of Manchester](https://www.humanities.manchester.ac.uk/social-statistics/research/postgraduate-research/current-phd-students/). My research addresses a critical challenge in public health and policy: how can we know if an intervention that works in a controlled trial will also work for a real-world population?

To solve this, I develop and apply rigorous causal inference and machine learning frameworks to bridge the evidence gap between experimental and observational data. My thesis develops a causal statistical framework to generalize the effects of food labelling interventions by integrating data from online RCTs with large-scale, complex UK nutritional surveys. I use methods like doubly robust machine learning estimators (e.g., AIPW, G-computation), statistical matching, and reweighting to build these models. I am advised by [Dr. Yan Wang](https://www.humanities.manchester.ac.uk/social-statistics/research/postgraduate-research/current-phd-students/) and [Dr. Nick Shryane](https://www.humanities.manchester.ac.uk/social-statistics/research/postgraduate-research/current-phd-students/](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/n.shryane).

My research interests include causal inference, quantitative policy evaluation, experimental design, complex survey design, and epidemiological research. I am passionate about using these advanced statistical methods to create robust, data-driven evidence for better decision-making in public health and social policy.

Before starting my PhD, I had a decade-long career in public service and data analysis in Chile. I served as the Chief of the Department of Agriculture Statistics at the National Institute of Statistics, where I directed the national agricultural statistics program and led a multidisciplinary team of 15 statisticians, data analysts, and IT staff. My prior experience also includes coordinating large-scale longitudinal surveys and working as a data analyst on program impact evaluation for the Chilean Ministry of Art and Culture. I hold an MSc with Distinction in Social Research Methods and Statistics from the University of Manchester , a BA in Economics , and a BA in Sociology.

I am always open to discussing new research, collaborating on projects related to causal inference and policy evaluation, or sharing insights on data leadership. Feel free to get in touch or browse my repositories to see my work.
