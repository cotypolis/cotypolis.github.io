Hello, I'm Constanza! 👋

I am a PhD Candidate in [Social Statistics at the University of Manchester](https://www.humanities.manchester.ac.uk/social-statistics/research/postgraduate-research/current-phd-students/). My research addresses a critical challenge in public health and policy: how can we know if an intervention that works in a controlled trial will also work for a real-world population?

My research program focuses on developing and applying rigorous causal inference and machine learning frameworks to evaluate and generalize health interventions. My core interests include:

* **Causal Inference & Transportability:** Developing methods to generalize findings from RCTs to real-world populations.
* **Quantitative Policy Evaluation:** Applying advanced statistical methods to estimate the real-world impact of health and social policies.
* **Nutritional Epidemiology:** Using causal and machine learning models to understand the complex, high-dimensional data of dietary patterns.

I apply rigorous causal inference and machine learning frameworks to bridge the evidence gap between experimental and observational data. My PhD thesis develops a causal statistical framework to generalize the effects of food labelling interventions by integrating data from online RCTs with large-scale, complex UK nutritional surveys. I use methods like doubly robust machine learning estimators (e.g., AIPW, G-computation), statistical matching, and reweighting to build these models. I am advised by [Dr. Yan Wang](https://ywangsoc.com/about) and [Dr. Nick Shryane](https://research.manchester.ac.uk/en/persons/n.shryane).

Before starting my PhD, I had a decade-long career in public service and data analysis in Chile. I served as the **Chief of the Department of Agriculture Statistics** at the National Institute of Statistics, where I directed the national agricultural statistics program and led a multidisciplinary team. My prior experience also includes coordinating large-scale longitudinal surveys and working as a data analyst on **program impact evaluation** for the Chilean Ministry of Art and Culture. I hold an MSc with Distinction in Social Research Methods and Statistics from the University of Manchester, a BA in Economics, and a BA in Sociology.

I am always open to discussing new research or collaborating on projects that leverage causal inference and ML to solve complex problems in health policy, nutritional epidemiology, and health equity. Feel free to get in touch or browse my repositories to see my work.
